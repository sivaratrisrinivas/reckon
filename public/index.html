PUBLIC RECORD

One prediction at a time

The question stays fixed: will the settlement price be strictly above the reference? Equality resolves to no. No prediction is created when inference abstains, evidence is stale, or a required provider is unavailable.

The oracle reports a bounded settlement-time observation, not a guaranteed historical price at the exact deadline. Caller timing and provider caching can affect that observation.
