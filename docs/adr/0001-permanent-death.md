# Death remains permanent after late winnings

Reckon treats exhaustion of spendable bankroll as permanent death, even when an unresolved challenge later pays winnings to the agent. New predictions and new challenges stop, while existing challenges still settle and pay correctly; late winnings and unsolicited funds cannot revive the agent. This preserves the consequence of running out of spendable funds instead of allowing a temporary shutdown followed by revival.

Accepted by the user during the grill-with-docs interview on 13 November 2026, IST. Upkeep is a configured economic rule, not a measurement of actual compute bills.
