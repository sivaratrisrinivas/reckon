# Retire upkeep without sink payments

Reckon's upkeep is an economic rule for a test-money experiment rather than a metered reimbursement of compute bills. It permanently removes funds from the usable bankroll while leaving them in the contract account, eliminating the sink beneficiary and asynchronous reimbursement payments. Retired funds cannot fund new stakes or revival and are described as retired rather than burned; actual infrastructure costs remain operational costs.

Accepted by the user during the grill-with-docs interview on 13 November 2026, IST. The live initial upkeep rate provides fifteen days of idle life before betting results; local demos accelerate the rate.
