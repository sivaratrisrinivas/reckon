# Request forecasts on demand

Visitors request a forecast with "Ask Reckon" when no prediction is unresolved, replacing the background publication schedule. The agent chooses its forecast or abstains, and published predictions proceed through their deadlines, settlement, and payouts automatically; upkeep continues between visits. This trades unsolicited activity for an immediately accessible forecasting opportunity without continually spending on unused predictions, with request cooldowns and an operational spending limit against spam.

Accepted by the user during the grill-with-docs interview on 13 November 2026, IST. The AI makes the judgment and deterministic rules govern stakes, while visitors initiate forecasting opportunities.
